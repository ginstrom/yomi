# Sprite provenance

Every PNG here is generated by
[sprite-tool](https://github.com/ginstrom/sprite-tool) from the text sources in
`art/` (`npm run sprites`), and is listed in `.sprite-tool-manifest.json`.
Change the source and rebuild instead of editing a PNG. The same build writes
`sprites.json`, an index of each sprite's size, anchor, bounds and display
scale, which the game does not load yet, and the atlas: `atlas.png`,
`atlas.json` and `atlas.anims.json`, every frame of every sprite on one sheet
with its animations, loaded by `BootScene`.

Units, what they hold and the world map's sprites are shown from the atlas.
Icons and portraits are loaded as the loose PNGs listed below. A sprite with
several frames also has a loose `<id>__<frame>.png` for each, and the `gear_*`
sprites have loose PNGs too; nothing loads those, and the build writes them
because it cannot leave a sprite out of a target.

The sprites are original to this project and share one palette
(`art/palette.toml`): muted steel, leather and torchlit stone, after the
Infinity Engine CRPGs (Baldur's Gate 1 and 2). No third-party art is included.
