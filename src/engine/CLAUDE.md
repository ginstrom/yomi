# The battle engine and the AI

Loaded when working in `src/engine/`. The root `CLAUDE.md` has the module map; `src/combat/CLAUDE.md` the stat blocks, spells and abilities the engine reads.

## The engine's contract

- `src/engine/battleEngine.ts`: the battle state machine. Callers only ever:
  - ask `legalActions(unit?)`, `previewMove`, `previewAttack`, `previewCast`, `previewAbility` (a use of an ability: its costs and the odds on each unit it touches, a strike's first blow's as `attack` rolls it, null where nothing rolls), `getState()`
  - call `step(action)`, which returns the `BattleEvent[]` it produced
  
  An `ability` action uses one of the unit's abilities, once `canUse` allows it (the ability held, its `needs` met by `isMelee`, its AP and mana there, not recharging; AP and mana are paid as it begins): a routine of strikes at a foe in its attack range, each strike an `AttackEvent` made as `attack` makes one, with the strike's dice plus `abilityDamageBonus` and its `ability`, `strike` and any `status` laid recorded on it; a technique's weapon strike is the same plain attack, the unit's own weapon, flanking, arc, the defender's turn, criticals, piercing and runes as ever, with the strike's `toHit` added to the chance, its `damageBonus` added after a critical's doubling, and its `onHit` entries after the weapon's (each with an element raised by the unit's `imbueDamage`), drawing as a plain attack does; a strike that `spreads` (Cleave's second) goes to another standing foe in reach, the nearest, chosen before its roll, else the same foe; a strike whose target has fallen goes to the nearest standing foe in reach (the lowest id on a tie), and with none it and the rest are not made and log nothing. The first `AttackEvent` of a technique that costs mana carries `manaCost` and `manaAfter`, as a cast does. A status ability (`selfStatus`, aimed at the user's own id, or `foeStatus`, at a standing foe within its range) draws nothing and logs one `AbilityEvent` on the target's hex with one outcome shaped as a friendly spell's (and `manaCost`, `manaAfter` for one that costs mana). Or it is an area aimed `at` a hex (legal on the board, the user's own for `centredOnSelf`, else a burst within range, the weapon's `attackRange` for `weaponRange`, a cone or line 1 to range away, touching a standing foe), one `AbilityEvent` resolved over everyone it touches (`abilityTouches`, which the policy reads too: both sides, or only foes for `foesOnly`) as a hostile area spell is, with the unit's own chance to hit (a critical doubling the dice): resistance ignored, or gone through as a shot's with `throughResistance` (`CastOutcome.resisted`), its element cut by wards, half the dice (rounded down, no status) on a miss where it has `halfOnMiss` (`CastOutcome.half`), the weapon's dice for `weaponDamage` (no `abilityDamageBonus`, no runes), the dice's count doubled against a unit `baneApplies` to for `bane`; an `auto` area draws nothing and lays its status on each unit touched (Taunt's `taunted` naming the user as its `source`), each outcome shaped as a friendly spell's; `cast` and it share `strikeUnit` and `aftermath`, so neither may change the other's draws. Each unit's `recharging` (on `UnitRecord` and `UnitSnapshot`, so in snapshots and saves) counts the rounds until a used ability is ready, a round coming off as its side's turn begins, after the ticks and regeneration; `legalActions` lists abilities after casts.
  Events are the engine's only output and are also the log format. **Bump `LOG_SCHEMA_VERSION` whenever an event's shape changes** (26 since the skill trees' attack traits: an attack's `traitBonus`, `counter`, `ambush` and `manaRestored`, with `deathblow`, `apRefunded` and `endured`, and `endured` on a `CastOutcome` and a `StatusTickEvent`, from the kill and survival traits, and the `restore` event (`RestoreEvent`) and `status_laid`'s cause `shadowdancer`, from the auras and turn-start restores; 25 brought the techniques' `manaCost` and `manaAfter`, `tauntPenalty`, `status_laid`). Events record more than the player is shown (AP spent and left, the numbers behind each roll, the items behind a gear change); `formatEvent` renders the short on-screen line, so add detail to the event and keep the line simple. An `AttackEvent`'s `melee` says whether the blow was struck with a melee weapon, reach included (`isMelee`), which is how the battle screen tells a swing from a shot. A hit that strikes no image adds the weapon's on-hit effects (`CombatantStats.onHit`, from runes): each entry's flat damage after resistance, cut by the defender's ward of its element and never doubled by a critical (a `bane` entry only against a unit `baneApplies` to), the wielder's heal, and its statuses (an `onCrit` one on a critical only) once the damage leaves the defender standing; none of it draws from the RNG. `damage` stays the weapon's; the rest is recorded in `bonus`, `healed` and `onHitStatuses`, and the defender loses `damage` plus every `bonus[].damage`. `battle_start` records the seed and policies so any battle can be replayed. `snapshot()` gives the whole engine as plain data and `BattleEngine.restore` takes it up again without starting anything over, which is how a battle is saved; it does not change the events, so it needs no bump.

## The AI

- `src/engine/policies.ts`: AI as pure functions `({ state, legal, rng }) => Action`, registered in `POLICIES`. The game's enemy and the simulator's two sides pick from the same registry. `aggressive` gives each unit the first of: Revive on a fallen ally whenever it is legal (`reviveCast`, kept from no heal reserve), a heal when an ally is at half its hit points or less, Purify on an ally asleep, frozen, poisoned, corroded or pinned (`purifyCast`), Mirror Image or Shield (or Dark Shield) when a foe stands beside it, Taunt (`tauntUse`) at half its hit points or more where it would taunt two foes not yet taunted, or one within its own reach of an ally worse hurt (by share) than the user, Mass Haste in its side's first two rounds where it touches two allies or more not hasted else Renew where two allies or more in its radius are at 75% or less and none is renewing, else Haste on the first ally in roster order, not itself, that is not hasted, else Protect on the ally with a foe beside it, not protected, the most hurt by share (`supportCast`), a hostile area spell that touches two foes or more and no ally (a spell with damage counting only the foes its average damage gets through the ward of; the Web spell one foe or more not yet webbed, and another spell that only lays a status, Bane, only the foes not under it yet), Bless on two allies or more, Glacial Prison (`prisonCast`) and then Sleep on the healthiest foe not asleep or frozen and with no unit of its own side beside it (whose blows would wake it) while two stand, Curse on the healthiest foe not yet cursed while two stand, Hunter's Mark (`markUse`) on the unmarked foe with the most hit points when it has the AP to mark and then attack, its hardest-hitting spell on the weakest foe (its damage reckoned with the caster's spell power by element, after the foe's ward, statuses and auras counted through `statsIn`, with Ice Lance's `bonusVs` dice where the foe has one of its statuses and Holy Light's doubled where its bane applies), then its best strike, the plain attack or routine of strikes that would do the most harm (each strike the chance to hit times an average hit through the foe's resistance plus its weapon's on-hit damage through the foe's wards, a bane only where it applies, a routine the sum of its strikes', and a routine winning a tie), or in its place a technique (an ability with `weaponAp`) whose `techniqueScore` is strictly higher: its strikes' expected harm (the strike's `toHit`, `damageBonus` and on-hit, `imbueDamage` included; a strike that `spreads` scored on the foe it would go to) plus each status it lays worth `STATUS_VALUE` (Seared the target's regeneration) times the chance, where the foe lacks it, less `MANA_WEIGHT` (0.5) a point of mana, all times the uses that fit in the AP left this turn (once with a recharge, no more than its mana pays for above the heal reserve) over the plain blows that fit (at least one), so a 7 AP Power Attack and a 5 AP blow at 8 AP are one against one; or in its place an area ability with damage whose net harm (each foe's chance to hit times the mean after its ward, plus half on a miss with `halfOnMiss`, less the same for its own side; the weapon's dice for `weaponDamage`, through resistance for `throughResistance`, double dice where a `bane` applies, less `MANA_WEIGHT` a point of mana) is above 0 and above that strike's; then an area ability that only lays a status with a roll (Web) where it touches a foe without it and none of its own side; then Sprint (`sprintUse`) when no foe is in reach and only Sprint's AP lets the closing move end with AP to strike; a caster that knows a heal keeps the mana for its cheapest, as does a mana technique. Taunt, Mark and Sprint are known by their data (an `auto` area laying Taunted, a `foeStatus` laying Marked, a `selfStatus` laying Hasted), not by id. A unit Taunted by a foe that stands keeps, in each step that has a hostile candidate on that foe, only those candidates, and closes on it rather than the nearest foe; every chance it reckons takes Taunted's penalty off (`tauntPenalty`). Monsters, which have no technique, choose as before step 12 but for one thing: since step 24 a caster of Bane (the Goblin Shaman, the Blight Horror) no longer casts it where it would touch fewer than two units not already baned; a snapshot of the encounters' choices in `policies.test.ts` holds them. It judges how close to come by a damaging spell of one foe it has the mana for, at that spell's AP to it (`spellApCost`), else by its weapon. It works over the cast actions `legalActions` lists and what `castTouches` says a cast would touch, so an enemy caster needs only a stat block with `magic`.

## Rules of the battle system

A side's units act in any order and the turn ends manually; units get 8 AP a turn by default, a move costs 1 AP per hex (`MOVE_AP_COST_PER_HEX`) and an attack costs the unit's `attackApCost` and reaches foes within its `attackRange`, both of which come from its weapon (`ItemStats`; the longsword costs 5 and reaches 1 hex, the bow costs 4 and reaches 5, so that a unit of 8 AP standing still shoots twice, and the light blades cost 4 and the dagger 3, for two blows; see the note over the weapons in `catalog.ts`); a melee weapon is one of range 1 or with `reach` (`isMelee`, which the engine asks wherever it tells a blow from a shot): the spear and the halberd strike from two hexes as melee weapons, with Melee Weapons, strength's damage, the melee perks and piercing, a range of 2 being an ordinary attack range; nothing blocks a shot or a spell, as there is no line of sight yet; a two-handed weapon and an off-hand item cannot be held together, so equipping either stows the other (`equipFromBackpack`), or drops it on the ground when the backpack has no room, and the inventory shows the weapon ghosted in the off-hand slot; stepping from a hex adjacent to a ready foe with a melee weapon, reach included, to one that is not provokes one free attack (a reach weapon's zone is still its neighbouring hexes only; a step to another hex beside it provokes nothing; one per unit per enemy turn, decided in `reactorsAt`, which `reachableHexes` asks about each step, and none at all for a mover with the trait `tumble`; ranged weapons, wands and the crossbow among them, hold no zone of control); a unit with the trait `counter` whose free attack is ready, holding a melee weapon with the attacker in reach, answers a melee blow of the side in turn that misses it (an image struck is a hit) with a free attack at once (`AttackEvent.counter`, spending `reactionReady`), before a routine's next strike, and a counter that fells the attacker ends its routine; the attack traits (`CombatantStats.opportunity`, `rear`, `movedToHit`, `execute`, `critDamage`, `manaOnHit`, traits `ambush` and `deadeye`, derived from the nodes' effects) add to the chance (`traitToHit`, recorded as `traitBonus`: a free attack's `opportunity.toHit`, a rear blow's `rear.toHit`, `movedToHit` on an own-turn attack after a step (`UnitRecord.moved`), `AMBUSH_BONUS` on a unit's first own-turn attack of the battle (`attackedYet`), whose hit is then critical with no second draw) and to a hit's damage after the dice (`traitDamage`, never multiplied: `opportunity.damage`, `rear.damage`, `execute` against a foe at half its hit points or less before the blow, `critDamage` on a critical); a Deadeye's shot (`deadeyeShot`) pierces `DEADEYE_PIERCE` (3) more (`blowPierce`, `resistanceLeft`) and its critical triples the dice; a melee hit that strikes no image restores `manaOnHit` mana up to the maximum (`manaRestored`); none of these draws from the RNG, `previewAttack` and the policy's expected harm read the same helpers, and a unit without the fields plays as before; every damage a unit takes (a weapon hit with its on-hit damage, a routine's or technique's strike, each outcome of a cast or an area, a status's tick) reaches it through the engine's one `applyDamage`, where Unbreakable (trait `unbreakable`) keeps a standing unit that the battle's first lethal damage would bring to 0 at 1 instead, Shielded for 2 turns (`UNBREAKABLE_SHIELD`; `endured` on the unit and on the `AttackEvent`, `CastOutcome` or `StatusTickEvent`); Deathblow (trait `deathblow`, `deathblows`) fells outright a non-`blightborn` foe that a weapon hit striking no image leaves at a quarter of its maximum hit points or less, judged on the hit points the hit and its on-hit damage leave without Unbreakable (so a hit lethal by itself on a foe Unbreakable would keep standing is a deathblow too), before any status (`AttackEvent.deathblow`); a foe it fells does not endure (its Unbreakable is not spent, no Shielded, no `endured`), while spells, areas and ticks never deathblow; Blademaster (trait `blademaster`) gives back the whole AP of the unit's own attack or strike technique (a routine's, whichever strike) that fells a foe, once a turn (`refunded`, false again as its side's turn begins; `AttackEvent.apRefunded`, `apAfter` still as paid), never for a free attack or a counter, nor for an area; none of the three draws from the RNG; bonuses to the chance to hit, in percent, stack from flanking (`FLANK_BONUS_PER_ALLY`) and from the arc struck (`ARC_BONUS`, judged at range by the neighbouring hex the shot comes in over); defence has two axes: armour class (agility, shields, the breastplate a little, statuses) comes off the chance to hit, and `resistance` (what armour stops, and a monster's own hide or plates) comes off the damage of every weapon hit, as flat points, a hit that rolled anything doing at least 1 (`damageThrough`); a flat cut and not a share, so that it tells most against weapons that hit often and lightly, the dagger, the shortsword and the bow, which are the weapons for the lightly armoured; a melee weapon and the strength perks pierce points of resistance (`armorPierce`, `effectiveResistance`), most of all the heavy weapons, which are the answer to the armoured; nothing pierces armour class; a shot, a wand's included, pierces nothing but the crossbow's own piercing (`rangedPierce`; the strength perks' piercing is a blow's, never a shot's), and a spell's damage, a status's ticks included, owes nothing to resistance, nor does an area ability's, nor a weapon's on-hit damage (runes), which is warded instead; an on-hit `bane` (the Dawn rune) applies only against a unit with `regeneration` or `blightborn` (the Blight Horror), and Seared laid on a hit (an Ember rune of rank 3) stops regeneration as Fireball's does; `previewAttack` gives the resistance left after piercing, which the battle screen shows on the foe's nameplate as "−N dmg"; a unit with `regeneration` regains that many hit points as its side's turn begins, after its statuses' damage (a `regenerate` event), unless a status with `stopsRegeneration` is on it (Corroded, from Acid Arrow, which also takes 2 off resistance, and Seared, laid for a turn by Fireball and Flame Arrow); after regeneration come, in this order, each a pass over the side's standing units in roster order and none drawing from the RNG, Renewing (the `renewing` status's `regen`, 3, blocked as regeneration is), Sanctuary (trait `sanctuary`: 3 to each unit of its side within 2, itself included, one heal a unit, the first Sanctuary in roster order named as `source`), Serenity (`manaRegen`, up to the maximum mana) and Shadowdancer (trait `shadowdancer`: Mirrored with one image for 2 turns on a unit with no image left, a `status_laid` of cause `shadowdancer`), each heal or mana a `RestoreEvent` (cause `renewing`, `sanctuary` or `serenity`; a restore of 0 logs nothing), then the recharge count-down; a tick that ends the battle stops it before them; the battle's first turn readies the player's side before `battle_start` is logged and runs these after it (`readySide`, `turnBegins`); auras (`AURAS`, `auraBonus`): a unit gains, for each kind, the bonus of any standing ally of its side in reach, two of a kind not stacking: Guardian +5 armour class within 1 (not itself), Aura of Courage (trait `courage`) +5 to hit, spells' too, within 2 (not itself), Aegis +2 resistance and +2 ward against every element within 2 (itself included); `statsIn(units, unit)` is `effectiveStats` plus that, which the engine reads at every roll and damage (its `statsOf`), its previews and the policies, so the odds shown include auras; facing is automatic, set by moving, attacking and casting, and once each enemy turn a unit struck in its side or rear by a melee weapon, reach included, hit or miss, turns to face the attacker before the roll (a shot, a wand's included, or a spell gives no turn and spends none) (`turnReady`, readied with `reactionReady` and never for the side taking its turn, so a mover struck by a free attack cannot turn; `AttackEvent.defenderTurned`); a second attacker from another side then finds the arc that exposes, and previews, the AI and `planAttack` judge the arc by `arcStruck`, the front for a melee blow (`isMelee`) on a foe that can still turn.

## Rules of casting

See `docs/designs/spells.md`. A unit whose stats have `magic` may cast a spell it knows, for the spell's action points and mana, at what the spell is aimed at (`Spell.target`): a standing foe within range, a standing ally within range (itself included), itself, an area, a hex of the board within range (its own for a spell of range 0, Bless and the Heal Alls) whose radius touches every standing unit around it, or `fallen` (Revive): an ally at 0 hit points that fell this battle (`UnitRecord.fallenThisBattle`, set by `fellAll` and kept after it stands again; a unit that entered the battle at 0 never fell), within range (friendly range counting, as for an ally), on a hex no standing unit holds, which `castTouches` returns though it is down. The cast action names a `target` unit or, for an area, the hex `at` its centre. A hostile spell (offence, and Bane) rolls to hit each unit it touches, in roster order, as an attack does (`rollToHit`, then the dice on a hit, so the RNG is drawn from the same way), with the caster's spell skill for its chance and its own critical chance; flanking and facing add nothing to it: the spell is the reliable strike, the blade the one that rewards position. A hostile area touches everyone in it, the caster's side and the caster included, and is illegal where it holds no foe; a cast that fells the last of both sides loses the battle, as the player's side is judged first. A friendly spell never misses, touches only its own side and heals up to the maximum. A spell's area has a shape (`Spell.shape`, a burst when left out; a cone or line is aimed 1 to `range` hexes from the caster and reaches `range`), worked out by `areaOf`, which the scene also draws from. `castTouches` (over `areaTouches`) says whom a cast would touch, for the engine, the policies and the battle screen alike, and `previewCast` the odds on each. A spell or a status's tick may have an element (`Spell.element`, `StatusRule.element`: Flame Arrow and Fireball fire, Frost Blast frost, Acid Arrow and Corroded acid, Death Cloud and Poisoned poison, Lightning Bolt lightning), and a unit's ward against it takes its points off each hit (after a critical's doubling) and each tick (`warded`, recorded on the outcome and the tick as the points stopped, and logged as "(warded N)"); a status is laid whatever the ward stopped, and a tick warded to 0 wakes no sleeper. A spell with `drain` (Drain Life) heals its caster that share of the hit points its targets lost (none for damage past what a target had left), rounded down and no more than it had lost (`CastEvent.drained`). A spell with `bane` (Holy Light) doubles its dice count against a unit `baneApplies` to, so it draws more only there; one with `bonusVs` (Ice Lance: chilled, slowed, webbed, asleep or frozen) rolls those dice too on a hit against a unit that had one of the statuses before the roll, right after its own and doubled with them on a critical, power and the ward then applied once. A spell with `oncePerBattle` (Revive) is refused to a caster whose `UnitRecord.usedOnce` (saved in snapshots) holds it, so a unit revived and fallen again can come back only by another caster. Revive draws nothing: its target stands at max(1, ⌊maxHp / 4⌋), its statuses gone (no `status_end`), with no AP, no free attack and no turn to meet a blow, its `endured` kept, and its outcome says `revived` (`healed` the hit points it stands at); `checkWinner` is as ever, so a battle ends the moment a side is down and Revive can only come while an ally stands. A spell with `cleanse` (Purify) ends every status whose rule is `hostile` on its target, in the order laid, each logged as a `status_end` (reason `cleansed`) after the cast, then heals as any heal does. Lasting effects (`STATUSES`: asleep, chilled, corroded, poisoned, shielded, protected, blessed, baned, hasted, mirrored, seared, cursed, webbed, warded, marked (armour class −10), slowed (action points −3), pinned, taunted, rooted (armour class +10, resistance +2), renewing and frozen; each a foe lays to harm is marked `hostile`: asleep, chilled, corroded, poisoned, baned, seared, cursed, webbed, marked, slowed, pinned, taunted and frozen) are `UnitRecord.statuses`, and `effectiveStats` adds what they do to armour class, resistance, the chance to hit and, for Warded (`wardAll` 4), the ward against every element, which every roll reads (through `statsIn`, with the auras); Renewing (`regen` 3) heals as its side's turn begins. A status lasts its unit's own turns, counting the one under way if it is laid on its side's turn, and ends after the end of the last (`status_end`); laid again it takes the longer duration, and different ones stack. AP changes apply as the unit's turn begins, and at once on its own turn; damage over time is dealt as its side's turn begins, unit by unit in roster order (`status_tick`), and may end the battle there, so `startTurn` returns events. A `helpless` unit (Asleep, and Frozen from Glacial Prison; `isHelpless`) gets no AP, holds no zone of control and is struck with `HELPLESS_BONUS`; damage ends a status that `wakesOnDamage` (Asleep, reason `woke`) on a unit it leaves standing, and Frozen has none, so it lasts its turns whatever hits it; a hit on a mirrored unit strikes an image and draws no damage dice. The Heavy Knight's three (skill trees step 11; none draws from the RNG): Taunted names its taunter (`ActiveStatus.source`, which no other status carries; a newer taunter replaces it, the longer duration kept), and every hostile roll of a taunted unit (an attack, a free attack, a strike, each unit a hostile spell or rolled area touches) at a unit other than its source loses `TAUNT_PENALTY` (25), taken off with the other bonuses before the 5–95 limits (`tauntPenalty`, which `previewAttack` and `previewCast` read too) and recorded as `tauntPenalty` on the `AttackEvent` or `CastOutcome` only when applied; when the taunter falls, Taunted ends on every unit it taunted (`status_end` reason `source_fell`, after the falls and before the battle's end). Pinned is `immobile`: `reachable` gives the unit no hex, so it has no legal move, while its attacks, casts, abilities and free attacks are untouched. Rooted is laid by the rule of the trait `rooted` (`CombatantStats.traits`, from the nodes' `trait`, `traitsOf`): as a side's turn ends, before its statuses count down, each of its standing units with the trait whose `UnitRecord.moved` is false (set false as its side's turn begins, true from its first step; in snapshots, and on a `UnitSnapshot` only once true) gets Rooted for 2 turns, logged as a `StatusLaidEvent` (cause `rooted`), so 1 is left through the other side's turn; the first step of a move ends it (`status_end` reason `moved`) before any free attack that step provokes is rolled. A unit that falls loses its statuses, and none outlasts the battle. A cast turns the caster to its aim, ends a paid-for change of gear as an attack does, and provokes nothing. `legalActions` lists casts after a unit's attacks, also for a unit with too few AP to attack, spell by spell, units in roster order or an area's centres in board order. A caster's `magic` may carry modifiers from its nodes (`power`, `healBonus`, `extend`, `friendlyRange`, `apDiscount`, each left out when 0): power, by element (`all`, `fire`, `frost`), is added to each damaging outcome of its spells after a critical's doubling and before wards (never to a status's tick); the heal bonus to each heal before the cap; `extend` adds turns to the statuses its hostile or friendly spells lay (an ability's or a weapon's untouched); `friendlyRange` adds to a friendly spell aimed at an ally or an area of range above 0 (`spellRange`); and `apDiscount` comes off every spell (`all`) or a healing one (`heal`), never below 2 (`spellApCost`). All are flat and draw nothing, and monsters carry none. Mana is a battle's budget: `UnitRecord.mana` starts at `magic.maxMana` whatever the character carried in and never comes back in battle; what a winner has left is copied back to its character, to heal with on the world map until the next battle, a level or a day of camping fills it.
