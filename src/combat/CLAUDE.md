# Combat data

Loaded when working in `src/combat/`. The rules the engine applies to this data (attacks, casting, statuses, traits) are in `src/engine/CLAUDE.md`; read it before changing anything that moves a battle's outcome.

## The module

- `src/combat/`: percentile attack resolution (a roll of 1 to 100 hits when within the attacker's `toHit`, plus bonuses, less the defender's armour class, the chance kept between 5 and 95; a hit is critical when the roll is also within `critChance`), damage rolls and what a weapon hit does through the defender's `resistance` (`damageThrough`, `effectiveResistance`), monster stat blocks and what a level adds to them (`foeAtLevel(base, level, native)`, from the kind's native level: a level below it counts as it, armour class, chance to hit, damage (its abilities' too, as `abilityDamageBonus`) and a caster's mana and skill grow by the levels above it, and hit points are scaled so that a block is as written at its native level and grows as a level 1 block would from there), the yardstick a foe of a level should sit in (`foeYardstick`: armour class, chance to hit, hit points and mean damage a turn (the better of its weapon's turn and a routine's), from the ready-made fighter's measured growth, which a test in `encounters.test.ts` holds every monster kind to at its native level, each field outside it a named exception), the spells as the engine casts them (`SPELLS` in `spells.ts`: school, cost, what it is aimed at, range and area, whether it is hostile, its damage and its element, healing, drain or status, and `bane`, `bonusVs`, `cleanse` and `oncePerBattle`; what a caster's modifiers make of its cost and reach, `spellApCost` and `spellRange`, which the engine reads wherever a spell's AP or range is; and `STATUSES`, what each lasting effect does; nothing of the perk that teaches a spell; `SPELL_IDS` are the heroes' spells, `HeroSpellId`, which the skill trees' nodes and casting on the world map alone read (30, each taught by one node, `perkTeaching`: Web and Curse, which monsters cast too, among them, and the six special spells, Ice Lance, Glacial Prison, Purify, Revive, Renew and Holy Light; `isHealing` counts a spell that restores hit points and is no cleanse, so Purify, cast for what it ends, is no heal on the world map, in `autoHeal` or in the policy's heal choices and reserve), and `MONSTER_SPELL_IDS` the spells only a stat block's `magic` can name (Lightning Bolt, Drain Life, Dark Shield), `ALL_SPELL_IDS` both in that order), the elements (`ELEMENTS`: fire, frost, lightning, acid, poison, blight) and a stat block's `wards` against them, flat points off each instance of damage of the element (`warded`), the abilities (`ABILITIES` in `abilities.ts`, by id as spells are, which a stat block's `abilities` names: a routine of weapon strikes at one foe, each with its own dice, verb and maybe a status, or an area; each costs action points, and one with a `recharge` of N is used at most every N turns of its side; the monsters' four come first in `ABILITY_IDS` and set none of the heroes' fields; the heroes' techniques after them, taught by a node's `ability` (`knownAbilities`), may cost mana (`manaCost`), cost the weapon's attack AP plus `weaponAp` (`abilityApCost`, which every reader of an ability's AP goes through; plus nothing for the Spellsword Strikes, `SPELLSWORD_STRIKES`, of a unit with the trait `arcane_knight`), ask a melee or a ranged weapon (`needs`), strike with the unit's own weapon (`Strike.weapon`, with a strike's `toHit`, `damageBonus`, `onHit` and `spreads`), or lay a status with no roll on the user (`selfStatus`, Sprint) or a foe in range (`foeStatus`, Hunter's Mark), or strike an area with the heroes' `AreaAttack` fields: the weapon's dice (`weaponDamage`) and range (`weaponRange`) and through resistance as a shot (`throughResistance`, Volley), only foes (`foesOnly`), no roll, only a status (`auto`, Taunt), double dice against the bane-struck (`bane`) and centred on the user (`centredOnSelf`, Taunt and Consecrate)), and the seedable `mulberry32` RNG, which can tell where its stream stands (`state()`) so that a saved battle rolls on as the unsaved one would. A stat block's optional `magic` (mana, spell skill, spells known) is what makes a unit a caster; without it a unit behaves as it always did.
