# The simulator and the balance

Loaded when working in `scripts/`. Read it before tuning: the bands below are the user's, and a change that moves play re-records the refactor guard (`scripts/simulate.test.ts`).

## Single battles

Simulator flags: `--battles`, `--seed`, `--out` (default `logs/battles.jsonl`; `logs/` is gitignored), `--max-rounds`, `--player-policy`, `--enemy-policy` (`aggressive` or `random`), `--player-weapon` (the id of any weapon `itemById` builds, of any family and tier, enchanted, graded or modified, e.g. `yew_bow`, `steel_war_hammer`, `iron_spear`, `iron_sword@2`, `iron_sword+light1` or, runed, `'iron_sword@3#ember1'` (quoted, so that no shell reads the `#`), put in every party member's hand; the way to compare weapon balance, between weapons of one skill, as the heroes are not all trained in the same), `--encounter` (an id from `ENCOUNTERS`, default `goblin_raid`), `--threat` (how dangerous to make it, default 1) and `--party-level` (the level every member is raised to, at full hit points and with its skill points and perks spent by `autoAdvance`, default 1); the last three check one cell of scaling. `--plans role=plan[,role=plan...]` (the roles `melee`, `archery`, `magic`, the plans `PLAN_IDS`; unknown or repeated entries are refused) sets the plan the player's heroes follow, by the skill a hero is tagged in, in place of `planFor`'s, in single battles and campaigns alike: `playerPlan` is handed to every `autoAdvance` of a player hero (raising the party to `--party-level` (`raisedParty`), levels after a win, recruits, guards formed), and never reaches the enemy, whose bandits keep their role's plan; it is how the path-parity cells run, e.g. `--encounter bandit_camp --threat 4 --party-level 8 --plans melee=swordmaster,archery=ranger,magic=cryomancer`. `--ready-level` (campaign only) is the level every member must reach before the party marches on the Source, default `SOURCE_READY_LEVEL` (7). `--parties` (campaign only, 1 to `MAX_PARTIES`, default 3; the refactor guard and the early-game figure use 1, with which no guard is ever formed and the play is stage 5's) is how many parties the company may field, a vanguard and home guards, the hall raised only as far as they use (`HALL_FOR_PARTIES`: 3, 4, 5, which `hallErrand`, `visitGuildHall` and `materialNeeds` keep to). A guard is formed at the hall (`formGuard`, from the reserves and recruits at the hall's level) when the hall allows another party, the company fields fewer than `--parties` and three heroes can be had, whenever the vanguard is home for another errand (it never walks home only for that: a rule that did, measured in stage 6's last step, cost walking days for no more wins); while one exists the vanguard no longer goes home to fill an empty place. A guard has its own policy (`decideGuard`, its choice `guardChoice`): reinforce (meet a vanguard short of its limit when it has two heroes or more, one within 3 levels of the vanguard's lowest, and promote its highest-level hero there by `exchange`; or meet a full vanguard whose lowest its best outranks by `SWAP_LEVELS` (2) or more and swap the two; either way a promotion, then go home), defend (the nest pushing hardest on a threatened held site), train (the nearest nest), each of a threat no higher than its lowest level and never the Source, garrison (the threatened held resource site with the fewest days to fall), else home and idle (and home first to refill a guard worn below three while a recruit can be had), running the hall there; no two parties' plans share a nest (the vanguard's `nextTarget` skips a guard's) or a garrisoned site. A garrisoned guard is asked again after every `advance` (`needsDecision`), and with no walk, meet, camp or day order running `advance` passes at most `IDLE_DAYS` (10) (`advanceLimit`). A wiped vanguard is replaced by the strongest party left (highest lowest level, then mean, then lowest id; `strongestParty`). The player's side is the ready-made party (`readyMadeParty`). It writes every event as JSONL and prints a summary of win rate, rounds, hit rates, free attacks, spells cast, area attacks used, hit points healed (a drain's included) and statuses laid by each side, and the techniques (the heroes' abilities, the four monsters' not among them) each side used by ability id (a routine of strikes counts once), the units taunted, the units pinned, the mana spent on techniques, and the stage-3 traits' counter-attacks and deathblows (the striker's side), revives (the caster's) and Unbreakable saves (the side of the unit kept standing), a battle (printed under the table, and after a campaign's; a spell's and an area attack's damage count in the damage dealt of the user's side, and a status's damage at a turn's start in the damage of the side whose foe it hurt; the hit and critical rates are of weapon attacks).

## Campaigns

`--campaign` makes each of `--battles` a whole campaign, which is how scaling is judged: a new ready-made party sets out on a new world (seeded from the campaign's seed) with the blight spreading, and walks a day a hex, on the company's day loop (`company.ts`), to where `nextTarget` sends it: the nest pushing hardest on a held site under threat, else the nearest nest whose threat is no higher than its lowest level, and the Source once every member has the ready level (`--ready-level`, by default `SOURCE_READY_LEVEL`, 7) or nothing else is left. It fights each at its own threat, carrying its hit points and the mana its casters have left, taking `rewardVictory` after each win, spending it with `autoAdvance` and camping where it won until healed (casting its heals with `autoHeal` and camping, both again whenever an event breaks camp), until the Source is beaten, no hero is left (`fallen`: a battle not won wipes the party out through `wipeParty`, its gear and baggage left on the location, and the campaign goes on while any party or reserve has a hero, the reserves forming a new party at the encampment if the last was wiped), the encampment falls or `MAX_CAMPAIGN_DAYS` pass. Item loot is not used; rune loot is, taken after each win (`takeRunes`, from the seed the battle was fought with). It refuses `--encounter`, `--threat` and `--party-level`, tags each `battle_start` with `meta.campaign`, `meta.location`, `meta.day`, `meta.party` (the party's id) and `meta.role` (`vanguard` or `guard`, `roleOf`; `run.vanguard` names the vanguard), builds a watchtower wherever it stops after a win if the land and the stock allow, then an outpost on the nearest held site without one within `OUTPOST_ERRAND_RADIUS` (6) if the stock allows (`outpostErrand`), goes home to the guild hall when the stock affords its next level and no held site is threatened or affords a recruit for an empty place (`hallErrand`), where it raises the hall as far as it can, up to `HALL_FOR_PARTIES` by `--parties`, and fills the party with pre-rolls, renamed when taken, spent by `autoAdvance` (`visitGuildHall`), then sells the baggage and the materials beyond what it still means to buy (`materialNeeds`), raises the trading post, workshop and infirmary as the stock allows, buys each member, for its weapon, shield and armour, the dearest item of the same family the post sells (tier 2 from level 4) that the gold affords and costs more than what is worn (extracting the old item's runes first where the workshop may, else selling them with it), then at a workshop only fuses every Lesser triple, buys Lesser runes for the empty sockets the pouch cannot fill and embeds by `RUNE_ORDER` (weapons Ember, Storm, Stone; shields and armour Stone, Life; a kind's highest rank first) (`runecraftGear`), crafts on them by `PREFERRED_MODIFIERS` (`craftGear`) and raises the shrine with what is left (`visitFacilities`); after each win it also field-crafts one piece of gear if the stock allows, a day. Its time passes only through the loop (`playRound`): a policy (`decide`, the vanguard's, or `decideGuard`, over a `Plan` per party, kept by id) gives the party, whenever it is idle or its order was cut short, its next errand (the hall's and facilities', a battle), order (a travel order, given afresh with a new route after every interrupt, or the camp order) or day's act (a tower, an outpost, the field craft, chosen up front by `fieldCraft`), which `carryOut` does or, for an order or a day's act, gives as the party's order (a day's act through `orderAction`, a travel order through `startTravel`), and `advance` runs the orders' days, every party's together, with the same decisions as before the loop; and it prints how the campaigns ended (the Source beaten, the party fallen, the encampment lost, out of days), the days, battles, spawns, sites captured and lost and the land blighted at the end, the watchtowers built and lost, the shrine's level at the end and the outposts built, the trading post's, workshop's and infirmary's levels at the end, the gold spent on gear, the tier-2 items bought and the gold taken from sales and the items crafted (and the field crafts' days), the runes found, bought and fused, the gold on runes and the sockets filled at the end, the hall's level at the end, the recruits and the heroes dead (in battles won: the summary's line says a wiped party's are not counted), the hit points healed between battles and the days camped for them (a day any party camped counted once), the party's lowest and highest level at the end (the fallen party's, for a campaign lost in battle), the levels heroes died at, the battles it took the party's lowest level to reach 5, 10, 15 and 20 and how many campaigns did, the battles won at each threat, and the company's record (`CampaignRecord.roles`, by role the battles, wipes and each party's levels at the end; `wipes`, `promotions`, `vanguardWalkHomeDays` and `guardBattles`; `deaths` and `deathsAtLevel` are heroes dead in battles won, a wiped party's not among them, and `socketsFilled` is counted as the last party falls in a fallen campaign; the summary's per-role levels average only the campaigns that end with a party of that role, and it counts the campaigns ending with the hall at 4 or 5; `callings` and `capstones`, the vanguard's heroes at the end by calling (`calling`, a tie counting every tied path) and by capstone held (`capstoneOf`, `vanguardTallies`), which the summary totals with the share of campaigns whose vanguard holds a capstone), printed after the rest: `npm run simulate -- --campaign --battles 500 --seed 1` (about five minutes when nothing else runs, longer beside other work, so run one at a time; a log of some 70 MB).

## Balance

The balance is judged by `npm run simulate -- --campaign` (the measure is the share of 500 campaigns from seed 1 that beat the Source without losing the encampment; the ready-made party of three, no loot, growing by `AUTO_PLANS`) and by single cells (`--encounter --threat --party-level`, a party of the threat's own level, 500 battles). The bands, set by the user for the skill trees: one party (`--parties 1`) 215 to 250 of 500, the default company 220 to 260, `--ready-level 10` 370 to 420, at most 2.0 heroes dead and 243 to 297 days a campaign, GR3 at least 470, BC3 400 to 470, BC4 430 to 490, BC5 420 to 490, SN5 and SP5 410 to 480, SG7 0 within 40 and SG10 above 2. The skill trees' history, in one line a stage (plan `docs/implementation-plans/skill-trees/`, its Measurements table the full record): the guard before the trees was 230 of 500 (company 239, `--ready-level 10` 389, 270 days, 1.8 dead); trees and passives took it to 425, techniques to 449 (0.5 dead, 339 days, the Source at level 10 from 199 to 422), traits and capstones changed nothing (the plans reach them late; at `--ready-level 12` 473 beat the Source and 449 of 500 vanguards held a capstone, Unbreakable 627, Deadeye 452, Meteor 0, before Delwyn took the design's build); stage 4 made foes tougher instead of the skills weaker (the user's decision: `FOE_HP_PER_LEVEL` 0.7 to 1.3, bandits in Fine iron from threat 3, a fourth foe at the Spider Nest, the policy's Bane fix, the cryomancer's Ice Lance before Web) and raised the Blight Horror from 55 to 63 hit points. Skill trees stage 5 (the node-cost amendment, `docs/designs/skill-trees-node-costs.md`, steps 27 to 31: a node costs 1, 2, 3 or 4 perk points by its tier, 3 at creation and 1 a level, `autoAdvance` saving for the plan's next node) first took one party from 243 to 172 and BC4 from 462 to 364; step 30 put it back with plan orders (Aldric's Unbreakable at 13, Delwyn's Arcane Blood at 4, Heal All at 6, Fireball at 9 and Meteor at 15, accepted as departures from the design's 15 and 14), bandits at threat 4 in plain gear and `FOE_HP_PER_LEVEL` 1.3 to 1.15. The figures now (step 30, seed 1, 500 each): one party 234 in 286 days and 19.4 battles with 1.1 heroes dead a campaign, the company 236, both in band; `--ready-level 10` 434 in 509 days, over its band but below the 445 the user accepted; cells GR3 500, BC3 455, BC4 454, BC5 494, SN5 451, SP5 475, TL5 0, SG7 0, SG10 1 (out of its band, accepted by the user), F1 to F3 500, 500, 463. At level 7 and 10 almost no vanguard holds a capstone (none at the default, under 2% at 10); `--ready-level 12` is where they show. Path parity (every plan of `AUTO_PLANS` within 50 of its role's best at Bandit Camp threat 4, level 4) holds for every plan but the evoker, 54 behind the healer (accepted by the user: the path has nothing to buy with a fourth point); at levels 8 (Hobgoblin Warband) and 14 (Bandit Camp threat 7, the capstone cell, where each parity hero holds its capstone but the two assassins) the gaps are reported, not gated: at 14 the melee plans are within 24 of the best, and `rogue_archer` is 75 behind, the cryomancer 59, the saint 84 and the arcanist 444, which the user accepted as the three-hero party's and the AI's doing, foes shooting the softest hero and the mage carrying the damage. The refactor guard (`scripts/simulate.test.ts`) is a hash of the first 5 campaigns' events, so a change that moves play fails it and re-records it with the new figures.

## What a tuner needs to know

A campaign is lost in a single battle, at threat 5 (the party camps whole between battles), so each battle is judged on its own, and the Source's battle is where campaigns are lost: the Horror's hit points are the lever for it alone. Foe counts feed back through experience (every foe beaten is levels), so move them first and in small steps: a fifth foe at threat 5 is a cliff, a fourth at threats 3 and 4 hardly tells while the party arrives whole, and a count that gives threat 5 less experience than threat 4 is wrong; hit points a level are the finer lever (they were 45%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 130% and 115% at successive changes of hero growth). The Spider Nest is threat 5 only because its threat 4 was won 500 in 500 whatever its numbers. Gear matters little to the simulator, as its heroes buy almost no tier 2 and wear few runes (the breastplate's spell skill −5 on the mage is what moved it from 248 to 233 once, weight telling against a caster being the point), and the game as played runs easier than the tuned figure, and a player's party may be stronger or weaker than the ready-made one. Several parties change nothing on today's map (with `--parties 1` and the default company the same wins; the vanguard is self-sufficient, so a guard adds nothing to the Source's share). Retune when a hero's growth changes (the budgets of creation, skill points a level, perk points or what a node costs, a node, spell or technique, mana), the ready-made party or the party limit does, or healing does.
