# The simulator in full

The whole of what `scripts/simulate.ts` does: every flag, the campaign's policy errand by errand, the parties and guards, and every statistic the summary prints. `scripts/CLAUDE.md` is the short guide, with the balance bands and current figures; this is the detail behind it. The history of the skill trees' measurements is the Measurements table of `docs/implementation-plans/skill-trees/index.md`.

## Flags and single battles

`--battles` defaults to 1000 and `--max-rounds` to 200. Each battle plays through `playOut` (`src/engine/autoPlay.ts`), both sides on one policy stream (`seed ^ 0x9e3779b9`), and is abandoned when it passes `--max-rounds`.

Simulator flags: `--battles`, `--seed`, `--out` (default `logs/battles.jsonl`; `logs/` is gitignored), `--max-rounds`, `--player-policy`, `--enemy-policy` (`aggressive` or `random`), `--player-weapon` (the id of any weapon `itemById` builds, of any family and tier, enchanted, graded or modified, e.g. `yew_bow`, `steel_war_hammer`, `iron_spear`, `iron_sword@2`, `iron_sword+light1` or, runed, `'iron_sword@3#ember1'` (quoted, so that no shell reads the `#`), put in every party member's hand; the way to compare weapon balance, between weapons of one skill, as the heroes are not all trained in the same), `--encounter` (an id from `ENCOUNTERS`, default `goblin_raid`), `--threat` (how dangerous to make it, default 1) and `--party-level` (the level every member is raised to, at full hit points and with its skill points and perks spent by `autoAdvance`, default 1); the last three check one cell of scaling. `--plans role=plan[,role=plan...]` (the roles `melee`, `archery`, `magic`, the plans `PLAN_IDS`; unknown or repeated entries are refused) sets the plan the player's heroes follow, by the skill a hero is tagged in, in place of `planFor`'s, in single battles and campaigns alike: `playerPlan` is handed to every `autoAdvance` of a player hero (raising the party to `--party-level` (`raisedParty`), levels after a win, recruits, guards formed), and never reaches the enemy, whose bandits keep their role's plan; it is how the path-parity cells run, e.g. `--encounter bandit_camp --threat 4 --party-level 8 --plans melee=swordmaster,archery=ranger,magic=cryomancer`. `--ready-level` (campaign only) is the level every member must reach before the party marches on the Source, default `SOURCE_READY_LEVEL` (7). `--parties` (campaign only, 1 to `MAX_PARTIES`, default 3; the refactor guard and the early-game figure use 1, with which no guard is ever formed and the play is stage 5's) is how many parties the company may field, a vanguard and home guards, the hall raised only as far as they use (`HALL_FOR_PARTIES`: 3, 4, 5, which `hallErrand`, `visitGuildHall` and `materialNeeds` keep to). A guard is formed at the hall (`formGuard`, from the reserves and recruits at the hall's level) when the hall allows another party, the company fields fewer than `--parties` and three heroes can be had, whenever the vanguard is home for another errand (it never walks home only for that: a rule that did, measured in stage 6's last step, cost walking days for no more wins); while one exists the vanguard no longer goes home to fill an empty place. A guard has its own policy (`decideGuard`, its choice `guardChoice`): reinforce (meet a vanguard short of its limit when it has two heroes or more, one within 3 levels of the vanguard's lowest, and promote its highest-level hero there by `exchange`; or meet a full vanguard whose lowest its best outranks by `SWAP_LEVELS` (2) or more and swap the two; either way a promotion, then go home), defend (the nest pushing hardest on a threatened held site), train (the nearest nest), each of a threat no higher than its lowest level and never the Source, garrison (the threatened held resource site with the fewest days to fall), else home and idle (and home first to refill a guard worn below three while a recruit can be had), running the hall there; no two parties' plans share a nest (the vanguard's `nextTarget` skips a guard's) or a garrisoned site. A garrisoned guard is asked again after every `advance` (`needsDecision`), and with no walk, meet, camp or day order running `advance` passes at most `IDLE_DAYS` (10) (`advanceLimit`). A wiped vanguard is replaced by the strongest party left (highest lowest level, then mean, then lowest id; `strongestParty`). The player's side is the ready-made party (`readyMadeParty`). It writes every event as JSONL and prints a summary of win rate, rounds, hit rates, free attacks, spells cast, area attacks used, hit points healed (a drain's included) and statuses laid by each side, and the techniques (the heroes' abilities, the four monsters' not among them) each side used by ability id (a routine of strikes counts once), the units taunted, the units pinned, the mana spent on techniques, and the stage-3 traits' counter-attacks and deathblows (the striker's side), revives (the caster's) and Unbreakable saves (the side of the unit kept standing), a battle (printed under the table, and after a campaign's; a spell's and an area attack's damage count in the damage dealt of the user's side, and a status's damage at a turn's start in the damage of the side whose foe it hurt; the hit and critical rates are of weapon attacks).

## Campaigns

`--campaign` makes each of `--battles` a whole campaign, which is how scaling is judged: a new ready-made party sets out on a new world (seeded from the campaign's seed) with the blight spreading, and walks a day a hex, on the company's day loop (`company.ts`), to where `nextTarget` sends it: the nest pushing hardest on a held site under threat, else the nearest nest whose threat is no higher than its lowest level, and the Source once every member has the ready level (`--ready-level`, by default `SOURCE_READY_LEVEL`, 7) or nothing else is left. It fights each at its own threat, carrying its hit points and the mana its casters have left, taking `rewardVictory` after each win, spending it with `autoAdvance` and camping where it won until healed (casting its heals with `autoHeal` and camping, both again whenever an event breaks camp), until the Source is beaten, no hero is left (`fallen`: a battle not won wipes the party out through `wipeParty`, its gear and baggage left on the location, and the campaign goes on while any party or reserve has a hero, the reserves forming a new party at the encampment if the last was wiped), the encampment falls or `MAX_CAMPAIGN_DAYS` pass. Item loot is not used; rune loot is, taken after each win (`takeRunes`, from the seed the battle was fought with). It refuses `--encounter`, `--threat` and `--party-level`, tags each `battle_start` with `meta.campaign`, `meta.location`, `meta.day`, `meta.party` (the party's id) and `meta.role` (`vanguard` or `guard`, `roleOf`; `run.vanguard` names the vanguard), builds a watchtower wherever it stops after a win if the land and the stock allow, then raises a held resource a level (`improveErrand`): of the held resources (never the encampment) within `IMPROVE_ERRAND_RADIUS` (6) whose next level the Builders' Lodge allows (`IMPROVEMENT_LODGE`: an outpost at any Lodge, works from 1, a fort from 2) and the stock affords, the nearest whose next level is a fort and which would fall in under `FORT_FIRST_DAYS` (30) by `daysToFall` with the party where it stands, else the nearest (a tie to the first in `sites`), walking there (phase `improve`, then `build`) and acting `{ kind: 'improve' }` if `improveRefusal` allows on arrival, one level an errand, goes home to the guild hall when the stock affords its next level and no held site is threatened or affords a recruit for an empty place (`hallErrand`), where it raises the hall as far as it can, up to `HALL_FOR_PARTIES` by `--parties`, and fills the party with pre-rolls, renamed when taken, spent by `autoAdvance` (`visitGuildHall`), then sells the baggage and the materials beyond what it still means to buy (`materialNeeds`: every level left of the hall up to its cap, the shrine, the trading post to 6, the workshop, the infirmary and the Builders' Lodge up to `LODGE_LEVEL_LIMIT`, a watchtower, one of each improvement level the Lodge allows now, and a craft's materials for each place), raises the trading post (to 6), workshop, infirmary and then the Builders' Lodge (to `LODGE_LEVEL_LIMIT`, 2, where `raiseLodge` refuses until roads) as the stock allows, buys each member, for its weapon, shield and armour, the dearest item of the same family the post sells (tier 2 from level 4) that the gold affords and costs more than what is worn (extracting the old item's runes first where the workshop may, else selling them with it), then at a workshop only fuses every Lesser triple, buys Lesser runes for the empty sockets the pouch cannot fill and embeds by `RUNE_ORDER` (weapons Ember, Storm, Stone; shields and armour Stone, Life; a kind's highest rank first) (`runecraftGear`), crafts on them by `PREFERRED_MODIFIERS` (`craftGear`) and raises the shrine with what is left (`visitFacilities`); after each win it also field-crafts one piece of gear if the stock allows, a day. Its time passes only through the loop (`playRound`): a policy (`decide`, the vanguard's, or `decideGuard`, over a `Plan` per party, kept by id) gives the party, whenever it is idle or its order was cut short, its next errand (the hall's and facilities', a battle), order (a travel order, given afresh with a new route after every interrupt, or the camp order) or day's act (a tower, an improvement, the field craft, chosen up front by `fieldCraft`), which `carryOut` does or, for an order or a day's act, gives as the party's order (a day's act through `orderAction`, a travel order through `startTravel`), and `advance` runs the orders' days, every party's together, with the same decisions as before the loop; and it prints how the campaigns ended (the Source beaten, the party fallen, the encampment lost, out of days), the days, battles, spawns, sites captured and lost and the land blighted at the end, the watchtowers built and lost, the shrine's and the Builders' Lodge's levels at the end, the improvements built by level (outposts, works, forts) and the held resources at each level at the end, the gold by source (the sites' yields, the market's and sales), the waiting check's line (below), the trading post's, workshop's and infirmary's levels at the end, the gold spent on gear, the tier-2 items bought and the gold taken from sales and the items crafted (and the field crafts' days), the runes found, bought and fused, the gold on runes and the sockets filled at the end, the hall's level at the end, the recruits and the heroes dead (in battles won: the summary's line says a wiped party's are not counted), the hit points healed between battles and the days camped for them (a day any party camped counted once), the party's lowest and highest level at the end (the fallen party's, for a campaign lost in battle), the levels heroes died at, the battles it took the party's lowest level to reach 5, 10, 15 and 20 and how many campaigns did, the battles won at each threat, and the company's record (`CampaignRecord.roles`, by role the battles, wipes and each party's levels at the end; `wipes`, `promotions`, `vanguardWalkHomeDays` and `guardBattles`; `deaths` and `deathsAtLevel` are heroes dead in battles won, a wiped party's not among them, and `socketsFilled` is counted as the last party falls in a fallen campaign; the summary's per-role levels average only the campaigns that end with a party of that role, and it counts the campaigns ending with the hall at 4 or 5; `callings` and `capstones`, the vanguard's heroes at the end by calling (`calling`, a tie counting every tied path) and by capstone held (`capstoneOf`, `vanguardTallies`), which the summary totals with the share of campaigns whose vanguard holds a capstone), printed after the rest: `npm run simulate -- --campaign --battles 500 --seed 1` (about five minutes when nothing else runs, longer beside other work, so run one at a time; a log of some 70 MB).

The improvements and income in the record: `improvementsBuilt` counts the `site_improved` events by level (index level - 1: outposts, works, forts); `improvementLevels` the held resources (the encampment not among them) at each level 0 to 3 at the end; `lodgeLevel` the Builders' Lodge at the end; `goldBySource` the gold of the `yield` events by source (`sites`, the yields without a `source`; `market`, the trading post's from level 4) and `sales`, equal to `goldFromSales`; `incomePerDay` the yields' value (`yieldValue`: gold, and every other material at `MATERIALS_PER_GOLD`, 2, to the gold piece; sales left out) over the campaign's `days` (its last world turn); and `waitingIncomePerDay` the same for the **waiting twin** (`waitingIncome`, the reference's A9): a game of its own from the same seed and starting party, its company idle at the encampment, passing days (`passDay`) until its turn reaches the campaign's `days` or its encampment falls, divided by `days` all the same; it buys, sells and fights nothing, and shares no game, stream or battle id with the campaign. The twin costs about a third again of a campaign's time (100 campaigns of `--parties 1`, seed 1: 85 s of CPU without it, 123 s with it), so it runs only with `--waiting` (campaign only; refused without `--campaign`), and without it `waitingIncomePerDay` is 0 and the summary leaves out its line, "Waiting check: income 1.3 a day campaigning, 1.0 waiting at home: waiting earns less" (or "waiting earns as much or more", `waitingLine`). `simulate.test.ts` checks A9 on seed 1's first 120 days, the smallest span at which campaigning out-earns waiting there (they earn alike up to about day 90, the party's yields then the home valley's alone).

## What a tuner needs to know, in full

A campaign is lost in a single battle, at threat 5 (the party camps whole between battles), so each battle is judged on its own, and the Source's battle is where campaigns are lost: the Horror's hit points are the lever for it alone. Foe counts feed back through experience (every foe beaten is levels), so move them first and in small steps: a fifth foe at threat 5 is a cliff, a fourth at threats 3 and 4 hardly tells while the party arrives whole, and a count that gives threat 5 less experience than threat 4 is wrong; hit points a level are the finer lever (they were 45%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 130% and 115% at successive changes of hero growth). The Spider Nest is threat 5 only because its threat 4 was won 500 in 500 whatever its numbers. Gear matters little to the simulator, as its heroes buy almost no tier 2 and wear few runes (the breastplate's spell skill −5 on the mage is what moved it from 248 to 233 once, weight telling against a caster being the point), and the game as played runs easier than the tuned figure, and a player's party may be stronger or weaker than the ready-made one. Several parties change nothing on today's map (with `--parties 1` and the default company the same wins; the vanguard is self-sufficient, so a guard adds nothing to the Source's share). Retune when a hero's growth changes (the budgets of creation, skill points a level, perk points or what a node costs, a node, spell or technique, mana), the ready-made party or the party limit does, or healing does.
