## 2. Requirements and context

- **S11-R1:** each kind's block, abilities, wards, `FOE_XP`, `FOE_NATIVE_LEVEL`, trophy and sprite.
- **S11-R2:** each encounter with its band, counts, fixed nests and spawn kind.
- **S11-R4:** the golem's immunity flag (built in step 03; this step gives it to the golem).
- **S11-R5:** trophies from kills (the Young Dragon's drake scale).
- **Design §2:** monster wards follow their element: the Young Dragon has fire 10, the golem poison 99. The Stone Golem's immunity to ticking statuses is `CombatantStats.immune`, checked when a status is laid. Rune loot favours a nest's element (gear §4.5).
- **Design §4, ring 15:** a Young Dragon's fire breath scorches the front rank. A party with Ember wards shrugs off a third of it. Its scales forge drake-scale leather.
- **Campaign §5.5 rows:** Stone Golem, native 14, wall, res 8 (a piercing check), Slam, immune to statuses that tick. Blight Cultist, native 14, caster, res 1, Lightning Bolt, Curse, Heal. Young Dragon, native 15, boss of its nest, res 4, Fire Breath (cone 3, 6d6, half on miss, recharge 3), routine Bite, Claw, Claw.
- **Campaign §4.2 (a Young Dragon on its turn):** if its Fire Breath has recharged, the policy considers aiming it; a cone 3 hexes toward an aim hex; it rolls its to-hit against each unit's armour class; a hit deals 6d6+L÷2 (here: 6d6 plus `abilityDamageBonus`, today's area rule), ignoring resistance, and lays Seared; a miss deals half; then it recharges for 3 rounds. Otherwise it closes and uses its routine, Bite (2d8) and two Claws (1d8 each), each a weapon attack with flanking, arc and resistance as usual.
- **Gear §4.6:** a unit's ward against an element is subtracted from each instance of damage of that element (an area's outcome after half on a miss), floor 0. A fire drake has fire 10; the golem has poison 99.
- **Design §6 per ring:** the kinds' yardstick test; the encounters' composition at their bands' ends; one engine test per kind with something special; the cells at both band ends with `--player-kit expected`.
